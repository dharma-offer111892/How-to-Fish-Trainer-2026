## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **How to Fish**. This trainer enhances the game's fishing and progression loop with options for money, gear purchases, creature encounters, combat survivability, and island progression. It is designed for single-player sessions and offline experimentation.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with fishing rewards, equipment, creature fights, rare catches, quests, boss encounters, and island progression without repeating the standard progression loop.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move around each island to find fishing areas, shops, quest interactions, and encounter locations. Use the movement options only when necessary, since extreme changes can interfere with physics-based interactions and positioning.

### Tools & Inventory

The game begins with limited equipment. Earn money by catching and selling fish, then purchase better fishing gear and weapons. Use the trainer's money and purchase options before buying equipment, and allow the game to finish each transaction before opening another menu.

### World & Environment

Progress through the islands by completing quests and defeating bosses. Later areas contain more dangerous creatures and improved equipment. Island unlock options are best activated from a stable gameplay state, preferably after returning to normal movement and closing active menus.

### Combat & Weapons

Fish and other creatures can be defeated with fishing equipment, melee attacks, or firearms depending on the situation. Boss encounters are required for normal progression. Activate health protection before entering a fight, and use one-hit or unlimited-use options carefully during physics-heavy encounters.
